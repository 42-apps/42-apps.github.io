        ✈ Global Flight Map

        An interactive 3D globe of the world's airports and the non-stop flights between them. Click any airport and every direct route it flies fans out as a glowing arc. Click a route to see exactly which airline — or airlines — fly that city-pair non-stop, plus the great-circle distance and an estimated flight time.

        Open Longest flights to rank the world's longest non-stop passenger routes and trace them across the planet.

        The airport & route network is a 2026 snapshot covering ~3,800 airports and ~30,000 non-stop city-pairs, with the operating airlines and average flight time for each. Data via flightsfrom.com (compiled by the airline-route-data project); airline home-countries enriched from OpenFlights. Schedules change constantly, so treat it as a current best-effort picture rather than a live timetable. The Longest flights table is curated separately to a 2026 ranking. Only non-stop service is shown — no connections. Built with globe.gl & three.js. Not affiliated with any airline or airport.

        🌍 How to use

        • Click an airport (the glowing dots) to fan out every non-stop flight it operates.
        • Click a glowing line — or a destination in the list — to see the distance, estimated flight time, and every airline that flies that route non-stop.
        • Bigger, brighter dots are bigger hubs (more non-stop destinations).
        • Search any airport, city or country up top.
        • Longest flights ranks the planet's longest non-stop routes — click a row to trace it.
        • Drag to spin · scroll to zoom.
