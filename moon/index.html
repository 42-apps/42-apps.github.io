🌕 Living on the Moon

Three things in one little world:

  • 🔭 Explore — a high-resolution 3D Moon (NASA / LRO imagery) you can spin and zoom right down to the surface, with the maria, the great craters, every crewed & robotic landing site, and the spots humanity is eyeing for its first bases.
  • 🏗️ Build — drop dome cities, hotels, solar farms, ice mines and launch pads onto the surface and watch a colony come to life. The poles are prime real estate.
  • 🚀 Launch — fly a SpaceX Starship from a hi-res Earth to the Moon with a realistic timeline: liftoff, booster separation, trans-lunar injection, a three-day coast you can fast-forward, lunar-orbit insertion, and a powered landing.
