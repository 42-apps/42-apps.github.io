    🌍 Where is all the world's wealth?

    💰 World Money Map

    An interactive atlas of money & wealth for every country on Earth. Colour runs from bright gold (the most) down to near-black (the least); slate grey means no data for that metric.

    Pick a category (Economy · Wealth · Assets · Debt) and a metric from the bars up top — GDP and PPP, currency strength, total private & government wealth, millionaires & billionaires, average salary, the total value of real estate, gold, equities, sovereign-wealth funds, crypto, money supply, debt and net foreign position. Turn on History to watch GDP, PPP and government debt change from 1990 to 2025. Open the World Wealth dashboard to see how all of Earth's measurable wealth splits across asset classes — and who holds it.

    Figures are a curated 2025–2026 snapshot from public sources — IMF World Economic Outlook, World Bank, UBS Global Wealth Report, Forbes, World Gold Council, World Federation of Exchanges, the SWF Institute, Savills, Numbeo and others. Many wealth-and-asset figures (total real-estate value, household wealth, crypto and gold held privately, small-nation estimates) are best-effort approximations, not official statistics — treat them as orders of magnitude, not precise accounting. Built with globe.gl. Not affiliated with any of the cited organisations.
