# World Money Map

An interactive 3D-globe + flat-map atlas of the world's money & wealth, country by country — and a **World Wealth dashboard** of where all of Earth's measurable wealth sits.

**Live:** https://42-apps.github.io/money/

## What it shows
Pick a **category** and **metric** and the map colours every country from bright gold (most) to near-black (least):

- **Economy** — GDP, GDP per capita, GDP (PPP), PPP per capita, currency strength (price level)
- **Wealth** — total private wealth, wealth & median wealth per adult, # millionaires, % millionaires, # billionaires, average salary, government net worth
- **Assets** — total residential real-estate value, home price / m², official gold reserves, stock-market capitalisation, total business value, FX reserves, sovereign-wealth funds, crypto held, bank deposits, money supply (M2), total national wealth
- **Debt** — government debt ($ and % of GDP), debt per capita, external debt, net international investment position

Plus: **History** animates GDP from 1990→2025, a **World Wealth dashboard** breaks the ~$880T of global assets across real estate / bonds / equities / cash / gold / crypto and shows who holds each, full ranking/search, and a click-through profile for every country.

## Data
A curated 2025–2026 snapshot from public sources — IMF World Economic Outlook, World Bank, UBS Global Wealth Report, Forbes, World Gold Council, World Federation of Exchanges, the SWF Institute, Savills and Numbeo. Many wealth-and-asset figures are best-effort estimates, not official statistics — treat them as orders of magnitude. Built with [globe.gl](https://github.com/vasturiano/globe.gl).
