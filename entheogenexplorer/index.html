Global Entheogen Map

An interactive 3D globe of where the world's clinically-relevant psychedelics & entheogens stand legally. Pick a substance and every country is painted on a 6-level scale from legal to death-penalty — with a Medical-access lens built for the question that matters most: where can patients and researchers actually obtain it?

⚠️ Not legal, medical or travel advice. Drug law is genuinely complex — national vs state law, possession vs supply vs cultivation, religious/indigenous exemptions, and the gap between the statute books and real enforcement. These statuses are an approximate, hand-compiled snapshot that may be incomplete or out of date. Never rely on it for any decision — especially in severe-penalty countries, where the risks are life-changing. Always verify with a qualified local source.

How to read the colours

Each substance is graded on one scale, ordered by access: Legal → Medical → Decriminalized → Tolerated → Illegal → Severe / death-penalty (the near-black landmasses, outlined in red). Overview averages all substances into one “how open is this country” read.

Molecule & brain explorer

The 🧬 Molecule & brain effects button opens a spinnable 3D model of each substance (real PubChem structures) alongside a schematic of its primary receptor target and the brain regions it affects. The brain panel is a simplified educational illustration of mechanism — not a literal neural simulation.
