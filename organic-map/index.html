    🌱 Organic Food Map

    An interactive atlas of organic agriculture for every country and territory on Earth. Colour runs from bright green (the most organic) down to near-black (little or none); slate grey means a country was not surveyed.

    Switch metrics with the bar at the top: organic share of farmland, total organic land, number of organic farms, organic land per person, 10-year growth, and the organic retail market. Turn on History to watch organic farmland spread across the world from 2004 to 2023.

    Headline figures are the FiBL/IFOAM survey for the 2023 data year. The 2004–2023 time series is FAOSTAT's organic-area series (methodology differs slightly from FiBL, so absolute values can vary). A handful of small territories (French overseas departments, Channel Islands) are folded into their parent state or omitted. Built with globe.gl. Not affiliated with FiBL or IFOAM.
