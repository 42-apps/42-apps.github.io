GlobalTax
World Tax Rate Explorer

An interactive 3D globe of world tax rates. Set your income & currency, pick a tax type, and every country is graded green → yellow → red by its tax burden — plus a New-resident mode highlighting relocation tax regimes. Switch to ☰ Table to rank every country best → worst on all seven taxes combined, live as you move the income slider.

⚠️ Not tax, legal or financial advice. The figures are approximate headline rates for illustration and orientation only. They may be incomplete, simplified, or out of date, and they exclude state/local taxes, deductions, reliefs, treaties and your personal circumstances. Always do your own research and consult a qualified professional or the relevant tax authority before making any decision.

Where the data comes from

Rates were hand-assembled and then web-verified (≈2025/26) against public references — primarily PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries, KPMG, Deloitte, the OECD, and national tax authorities. The “top personal income” rate aims to include surcharges/cess that apply at the highest incomes. Progressive brackets exist for major economies; elsewhere a representative curve is derived from the top rate. Relocation-regime details were fact-checked the same way. These are not pulled live — they’re a fixed snapshot bundled with the app, so treat them as a starting point, not a source of record.

Privacy

GlobalTax runs entirely in your browser. It collects no personal data, needs no accounts and no special permissions. Map and tax data is served as static files from this site; nothing you enter is sent anywhere, stored, or tracked. Your income slider value never leaves your device.
